Inside the fold

Two angles on a ring give four periodic inputs. Twelve sine neurons turn those inputs into three coordinates of deformation. The surface ridges mark equally spaced samples around the ring.

Each slider adds one rank-one matrix to the input weights. Its two vectors choose an input direction and the neurons it affects. Negative strength reverses the edit.

Play path interpolates the weights between four programs and evaluates the network at each step. Compare origin shows the zero-edit surface from the same camera. The section plot measures the distance between corresponding points in model units.
